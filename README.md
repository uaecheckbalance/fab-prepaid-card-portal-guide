# FAB Prepaid Card Portal: Check Your Card Balance Online

The FAB prepaid card portal is the online place where cardholders can look up what is left on a prepaid card without visiting a branch. It is useful before shopping, travelling, or topping up, because it shows the current balance in one place.

Before you start, keep your card number and the mobile number linked to the card nearby. Most portal checks ask for a few card details to confirm that the person checking is the cardholder, so having them ready saves a second attempt.

To check through the portal, open the [fab prepaid card portal](https://uaecheckbalance.com/fab-prepaid-cards-enquiry/) guide, follow the portal steps listed there, and enter the details exactly as they appear on the card. Once the result loads, note the available balance and the date shown beside it, since a card balance can change after a purchase or a refund.

If the balance looks older than expected, wait a short while and check again, because a recent transaction can take time to appear. If it still looks wrong after a second check, use the SMS or app method in the same guide as a cross check, then contact the bank through the number printed on the back of the card.

Keep the card details private, never share a one time code with anyone who calls asking for it, and sign out of the portal when you finish on a shared device.
